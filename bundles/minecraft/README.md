# Survie

Reference bundle for Hou.la interactive gifts, targeting **Minecraft** servers over RCON.
Viewers either reward the streamer (items, buffs, elytra, totem) or make their life harder
(mobs, night, thunderstorm, dragon), ordered from cheap to premium across `ix_slot_01`…`ix_slot_30`.

This is the pack the [CONTRIBUTING guide](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) points at as a complete example —
so it is held to the same rules as any community submission: original square PNG artwork under
512×512 and 300 KB, non-destructive effects, no forbidden commands, one slot used at most once.

## Effects

All effects are `rcon` and are only ever *suggestions*: they run on the creator's own machine,
through the connector, against their own server. The creator can override or disable any of them.
Most are harmless; **TNT (`ix_slot_26`) and the dragon (`ix_slot_30`) can damage the world**, which
is why they sit at the top of the price ladder.
